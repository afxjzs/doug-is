How to read it: # is the episode's number in air order, 1 to 331. IMDb rank, Fan rank and Avg rank are its position across all 331 episodes, so they hold still when you filter. Avg rank comes from the mean of the other two ranks, re-numbered 1 to 331. Greener is better rated, redder is worse; the scale runs from the 5th to the 95th percentile of whichever source is selected.

Δ is IMDb minus fan score, so + means IMDb rated it higher and − means the fans did. Its outline color tracks the size of the gap, not its direction: green where the two audiences agree, red where they part company hardest. Sorting it runs from the most IMDb-favored down to the most fan-favored, so the biggest arguments sit at both ends.

Two scores, very different samples. IMDb ratings carry thousands of votes each. Fan scores come from Series Graph members and often rest on a couple hundred — a 10.0 off one vote is noise, not a verdict. Faded chips have fewer than 50 votes; drag minimum votes up to hide them.

Season and episode numbers follow IMDb, which counts specials and shorts inline, so late seasons run a few numbers ahead of other listings. Episodes that have not aired yet are left out. Data pulled from Series Graph on 20 August 2026.
